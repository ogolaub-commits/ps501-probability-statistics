How to play
  1. Read and think. Each task opens with thinking time. Nothing falls and no bombs drop. Use paper or a calculator.
  2. Start shooting. Press Space or tap "I'm ready". Tokens fall and the task timer starts.
  3. Fill the answer bar left to right. Shoot only the piece for the highlighted slot, in the order you would write it.
  4. Avoid wrong tokens. A wrong piece, or a right piece too early, jams your gun for a second and tells you the mistake.
  5. Shoot the bombs. Five landings fill the danger meter and cost one of your three lives.
  6. Earn upgrades. 4 correct tokens in a row give a power-up. 3 clean solves upgrade your weapon.
  7. Pass with 80% clean solves. Clean means no wrong shots. On an 8-task level that means 7/8. Timed-out tasks come back once at the end.

    How to play

    1. The task card shows a question. The answer bar below it has one slot per piece of the answer.
    2. Each task starts with thinking time. Nothing falls and no bombs drop. Work it out on paper or a calculator, then press Space or tap "I'm ready". The shooting timer starts then.
    3. Tokens fall from the top. Shoot the piece that belongs in the next slot. Answers fill left to right, the way you would write them.
    4. A wrong token, or a right token in the wrong place, overheats your gun for a second, resets your chain and tells you the mistake.
    5. Shoot bombs before they land. Five landings fill the danger meter and cost a life; the task restarts.
    6. Every 4 correct tokens in a row earns a power-up: Shield, Slow-Fall or Magnet. Three clean solves (no wrong shots) upgrade your weapon. Upgrades only help against bombs.
    7. Pass a level with 80% of tasks solved cleanly; on an 8-task level that means 7/8. Timed-out tasks come back once at the end.
